Smart India Hackathon 2026
College-Level Internal Hackathon Report

Comprehensive institutional documentation, participation analytics, evaluation panels, and nomination results of the internal hackathon conducted at United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj on 22 August 2026.

📍 Naini, Prayagraj, UP 📅 22 August 2026 🎯 77 Teams · 462 Students 🏆 45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted
01

Executive Summary & Objective

The Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon at United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj was organized as a flagship nationwide innovation initiative to foster technical excellence, product-building mindsets, and problem-solving capabilities among undergraduate engineering students.

Conducted under the patronage of Principal Prof. Sanjay Srivastava, Institute SPOC Dr. Dhananjay Kumar Sharma, Dean Student Welfare Dr. Manas Pandey, Faculty Coordinators Mr. Gaurav Narain Singh and Mr. Kushagra Dwivedi, and spearheaded by Student Coordinator Gautam Kumar Maurya (GKM) and Harsh Srivastava, the event successfully mobilized over 462 students across 77 teams.

03

Problem Statements Repository (37 PS)

A total of 37 national problem statements (PS01 through PS36 plus PS37 Open Innovation) were made available to teams. The challenges spanned high-impact national themes including Agriculture, Healthcare, Smart Education, Cyber Security, Clean Water, Renewable Energy, and Smart Automation.

05

Selection Results & Ministry Nominations

Following aggregate scoring based on novelty (25%), technical feasibility (25%), working prototype (25%), and presentation defense (25%):

45 Shortlisted Teams (Nominated)

Officially nominated by the SPOC office to the central Ministry of Education SIH Portal for national evaluation.

5 Waitlisted Teams (Standby)

Placed on official merit standby quota to step in if nominated slots become available.
