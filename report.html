SIH UIT 2026 — Event Report

Official Comprehensive Document of the Smart India Hackathon 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon conducted at United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj on 22 August 2026.

1. Overview & Objective

The Smart India Hackathon 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon at United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj was organized as an institutional initiative to identify, develop, and showcase innovative student solutions aligned with national problem statements provided by government ministries, departments, and industry partners.

Conducted under the institutional guidance of Principal Prof. Sanjay Srivastava, SPOC Dr. Dhananjay Kumar Sharma, and DSW Dr. Manas Pandey, the conclave served as the formal evaluation and selection process for national nominations to the Ministry of Education Central SIH Portal.

2. Participation Summary

The internal hackathon witnessed record participation across computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, electronics, and multidisciplinary engineering branches:

  • 68 Teams Registered: Enrolled through the dedicated SIH UIT digital portal.
  • 57 Teams Presented: Evaluated live before judging panels on 22 August 2026.
  • 500+ Student Participants: Multi-disciplinary 6-member teams with mandatory female representation.

3. Problem Statement Bank

A comprehensive repository of 37 Problem Statements was curated and made accessible to student teams, covering domains such as Agriculture, Healthcare, Smart Education, Clean Water, Renewable Energy, Cyber Security, Smart Automation, and Open Innovation (PS01 through PS37).

4. Conclave Day Execution (22 August 2026)

The hackathon was conducted over an intensive 8-hour offline session featuring:

  • Formal Inauguration Ceremony in the auditorium with faculty dignitaries.
  • Parallel presentation rounds across multiple evaluation halls.
  • Live software demo walkthroughs, mobile application testing, and hardware prototype inspections.
  • Detailed technical Q&A sessions by faculty evaluators.

5. Judging & Evaluation Panel

An 8-member multidisciplinary judging panel assessed all 57 presented teams based on a standardized 4-part rubric: Novelty & Innovation (25%), Technical Feasibility (25%), Working Prototype / Demo (25%), and Presentation & Q&A (25%).

6. Selection Outcome

Based on aggregate scores and category quota allocations:

  • 45 Shortlisted Teams were selected for official institutional nomination to the Ministry of Education SIH Central Portal.
  • 5 Waitlisted Teams were placed on standby in strict order of merit.

7. Student Coordination & Execution

The student-side execution was spearheaded by Gautam Kumar Maurya (GKM) (Head, Developers Club, UIT | Student Coordinator — SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon) and Harsh Srivastava (Student Coordinator — Operations & Logistics), supported by the 8-member student core team: **Abhinav Tiwari**, **Amit Dubey**, **Prabhat Pandey**, **Abhi**, **Praveen**, **Ayush Singh**, **Rohit Pal**, and **Yash Singh**, who managed 8 continuous hours of on-ground operations, room allocation, scoring sheets, and technical setup.

8. Conclusion & Digital Archive

The event concluded with an official Thanksgiving ceremony. This digital portal serves as the permanent institutional archive preserving all records, team submissions, photographs, and credential verifications for future batches, recruiters, and academic reviewers.
