Smart India Hackathon 2026

College-Level Internal Hackathon Conclave Report

Comprehensive official report documenting the end-to-end execution, student participation demographics, problem statement allocations, 8-panel faculty judging deliberations, selection outcomes, and organizing leadership at United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj on 22 August 2026.

📍 UIT Campus, Naini, Prayagraj 📅 22 August 2026 👥 77 Teams · 462 Students 🏆 45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted
01

Executive Summary & Institutional Vision

The Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is the Government of India’s premier nationwide initiative launched by the Ministry of Education’s Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE to identify problem-solvers, promote out-of-the-box thinking, and engage youth in solving real-world challenges posed by Union Ministries, State Departments, and Industry Leaders.

In alignment with this national mandate, United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj conducted the SIH 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon to rigorously evaluate and filter the institution's top engineering innovations. Under the guidance of Principal Prof. Sanjay Srivastava, Institute SPOC Dr. Dhananjay Kumar Sharma, Dean Student Welfare Dr. Manas Pandey, and Faculty Coordinators Mr. Gaurav Narain Singh and Mr. Kushagra Dwivedi, the conclave mobilized 462 students across 77 multi-disciplinary teams.

05

Standardized Evaluation Rubric (100 Marks)

1. Novelty & Innovation (25%)

Uniqueness of the proposed approach vs existing market solutions.

2. Technical Feasibility (25%)

Architecture, chosen tech stack, and practical implementation depth.

3. Working Demo / Prototype (25%)

Live demonstration, UI responsiveness, and functional code readiness.

4. Presentation & Q&A (25%)

Clarity of slides, team coordination, and answering jury questions.
