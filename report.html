01

Executive Summary & Institutional Vision

The Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is the Government of India’s premier nationwide initiative launched by the Ministry of Education’s Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE to identify problem-solvers, promote out-of-the-box thinking, and engage youth in solving real-world challenges posed by Union Ministries, State Departments, and Industry Leaders.

In alignment with this national mandate, United Institute of Technology (UIT), Prayagraj conducted the SIH 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon to rigorously evaluate and filter the institution's top engineering innovations. Under the executive leadership of Principal Prof. Sanjay Srivastava, Institute SPOC Dr. Dhananjay Kumar Sharma, Dean Student Welfare Dr. Manas Pandey, and Faculty Coordinators Mr. Gaurav Narain Singh and Mr. Kushagra Dwivedi, the conclave mobilized 462 students across 77 multi-disciplinary teams.

06

Standardized Evaluation Rubric (100 Marks Framework)

Evaluations were conducted using a standardized 5-parameter rubric aligned with national SIH criteria:

1. Problem Depth (20 pts)

Relevance, domain research, and problem-solution alignment.

2. Innovation (25 pts)

Novelty and distinct competitive advantage over existing tools.

3. Tech Architecture (25 pts)

Scalability, database design, framework choices, and code quality.

4. Working Demo (20 pts)

Live operational demonstration, responsiveness, and hardware rig.

5. Team Defense (10 pts)

Clarity of presentation and technical Q&A defense before judges.
