Instructions for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Internal Registration

Welcome to the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Internal Registration Portal of United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj.

Please read the following instructions carefully before filling out the registration form.

Important Instructions

  1. This registration is only for the college-level internal registration process for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
  2. Submission of this form does not guarantee selection for Smart India Hackathon 2026. Teams will be shortlisted through the college's internal evaluation process.
  3. The official SIH 2026 Problem Statements have not yet been released. Further information regarding problem statement selection, team verification, mentoring, and the official SIH registration process will be communicated after their release.
  4. Students are advised to form their teams in advance. SIH 2026 mandates a team size of exactly 6 members (1 Team Leader + 5 Members).
  5. Each team must include at least one female member. This is mandatory for SIH 2026 internal registration.
  6. The Team Leader must complete this registration form on behalf of the entire team.
  7. Ensure that the details of all team members are entered correctly. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in rejection during verification.
  8. Each student should be a member of only one team. Multiple registrations using the same participant details may be considered invalid.
  9. Use a valid and active Personal Email ID and WhatsApp Number, as all official communication regarding SIH will be shared through these channels.
  10. Verify all information carefully before submitting the form. Once submitted, modifications may not be possible.
  11. The registration must be completed before the announced deadline. Late submissions may not be considered.
  12. The information collected through this form will be used only for:
    • Internal registration and participant verification.
    • Team formation and validation.
    • Mentor allocation.
    • College-level screening and evaluation.
    • Official communication related to Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
  13. Students are encouraged to regularly visit the official Smart India Hackathon website for the latest announcements and problem statement releases: https://www.sih.gov.in/

Declaration

By submitting this form, the Team Leader confirms that:

  • All information provided is true and accurate.
  • All team members have consented to participate.
  • The team agrees to follow the rules and guidelines issued by the college and Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
  • The team understands that this is an internal registration process and that final participation is subject to the college's selection process and the official SIH guidelines.

We wish all participants the very best for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
