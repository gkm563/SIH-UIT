Essential SIH 2026 Rules & Quota Constraints

Critical national guidelines established by the Smart India Hackathon central organizing team.

⏳ 500 Ideas Limit / PS Freeze

Idea submission counter starts in August 2026. Only 500 ideas will be accepted nationwide for a particular Problem Statement. Once 500 submissions are reached, that PS gets immediately frozen.

📊 Live ideas submitted & left counter is visible on sih.gov.in
🎯 Max 2 Problem Statements

One nominated team can submit ideas against a maximum of 2 Problem Statements only (or Student Innovation Category). Software and Hardware tracks have no category restriction — combine as convenient.

💡 Total Institute Quota: 50 Teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted)
🔑 SPOC Institutional Nomination

Students cannot register directly on the SIH portal. College SPOC handles nominations. The Nominated Team Leader will receive central portal credentials directly on their registered Email / Mobile.

📧 Team Leader must check inbox & SMS regularly
