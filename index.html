❓ Student Participant FAQ & Essential Guidelines

Rule 1 Team Composition & Mandatory Rules

• Team Size: Exactly 6 members per team (1 Team Leader + 5 Members). All 6 members must belong to United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj.

• Mandatory Female Member: At least ONE female member is strictly compulsory in every team.

• Multi-Disciplinary Mix: For Software edition, strong coding skills are crucial. For Hardware edition, a mix of Mechanical, Electronics, Software, and Design skills is encouraged.

Rule 2 Internal Hackathon Presentation (22 August 2026)

• Event Date: 22 August 2026, Saturday (Full Day). Venue details will be announced soon.

• What to Prepare: Prepare your solution prototype and structure your presentation using the official SIH Idea PPT Template.

• Jury Evaluation: Teams will present their project live before internal college judges.

Rule 3 Selection Quota (Top 50 Nominated Teams)

• Institute Quota: The college will nominate Total 50 Teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waiting List Teams) directly on the official SIH Government Portal.

• College Authorization: Selected teams will receive an official college authorization letter signed by the Director/Principal for the national finale.

Rule 4 National Grand Finale & ₹1,50,000 Prize Money

• Grand Finale: Held in December 2026 across offline nodal centers in India with travel reimbursement up to ₹3,000/person.

• National Prize Money: ₹1,50,000 per problem statement awarded to the winning team by collaborating ministries/industries!

We wish all participants the very best for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
