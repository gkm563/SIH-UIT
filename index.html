📋 Official SIH 2026 Guidelines & Rules Breakdown

01 & 02 SPOC Role & College Level Registration

• Student Registration: Students cannot register directly on the national SIH portal. Your college Single Point of Contact (SPOC) coordinates all internal registrations and team nominations.

• Who can be SPOC: Any senior faculty member (Lecturer, Assistant/Associate Professor, HOD, Dean, Director, Principal).

• Internal Evaluation: Only teams selected through the College-Level Internal Hackathon will be registered by the SPOC on the national portal.

03 & 07 Mandatory Team Formation Guidelines

• Team Size: Exactly 6 members per team (1 Team Leader + 5 Members). No inter-college teams allowed.

• Female Representation (Mandatory): At least ONE female member is strictly mandatory per team.

• Multi-Disciplinary Mix: For Software edition, strong coding skills are crucial. For Hardware edition, multi-disciplinary skills (Mechanical, Electronics, Software, Design) are encouraged.

04 & 08 Team Nomination Process (Total 50 Teams: 45 Shortlisted + 5 Waiting)

• Maximum Quota: A maximum of 50 teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted) can be nominated by the institute on the official SIH portal.

• College Authorization Letter: The team nomination requires a formal letter on official college letterhead signed by the Principal/Director with the college seal.

• Unique Team Name: Team names must be unique and MUST NOT contain the name of the institute.

10 & 11 National Idea Submission & 500-Idea Cap Limit

• 500 Ideas Per PS Cap: Once a particular Problem Statement receives 500 ideas nationwide on `sih.gov.in`, that PS will be frozen!

• Max PS per Team: One team can submit ideas against a maximum of 2 Problem Statements.

• Final Submission Deadline: 15th September 2026 on the official SIH portal.

13 - 18 Grand Finale, Mentors & ₹1,50,000 Prize Money

• Grand Finale Date: Proposed in December 2026 across offline nodal centers in India.

• Travel Reimbursement: Maximum ceiling of ₹3,000 per person for round trip travel.

• Mentors: Each team can have up to 2 mentors from industry/academia (5+ years experience).

• Prize Money: ₹1,50,000 per problem statement for the winning team.

We wish all participants the very best for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
