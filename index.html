📋 Overview

From Registration to Evaluation

The SIH 2026 College-Level Internal Hackathon at UIT was conducted as the internal evaluation and selection process for student teams participating in SIH 2026.

01

Registration

Teams registered through the dedicated SIH UIT portal with verified 6-member interdisciplinary rosters.

68 Teams Registered
02

Problem Statements

Teams selected one problem statement from 37 central challenges and developed their proposed technical solutions.

37 Problem Statements
03

Internal Hackathon

Teams presented their ideas, prototypes, demonstrations and solutions before the 8-member judging panel on 22 August 2026.

57 Teams Presented
04

Evaluation & Selection

Following evaluation, 45 teams were shortlisted and 5 teams were placed on the waitlist for the subsequent nomination process.

45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted
