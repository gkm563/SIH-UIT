SIH 2026 National Round — Important Submission & Portal Instructions

Internal College-Level Hackathon has concluded. UIT is nominating the top 50 teams (45 shortlisted + 5 waitlisted) to the central Smart India Hackathon portal. Please review the mandatory instructions and checklist below.

⏳ 500 Ideas Quota / PS Freeze

Idea submission counter starts from August 2026. Only 500 ideas can be submitted nationwide for a particular PS. Once 500 ideas are reached, that PS will freeze and no more submissions will be accepted.

📊 Live counter (submitted & left) visible on sih.gov.in
🎯 Max 2 Problem Statements

One team can submit ideas against a maximum of 2 Problem Statements only (or Student Innovation Category). There is no bar for Software/Hardware categories — choose as per convenience.

💡 Total Institute Quota: 50 Teams (45 Shortlisted + 5 Waitlisted)
🔑 SPOC Nomination & Credentials

Students cannot register directly. College SPOC handles nominations. The Nominated Team Leader will receive portal login credentials directly from SIH on their registered Email / Mobile.

📧 Check registered Email & SMS regularly
📋

10-Point Checklist: Details Team Leader Must Verify & Submit on SIH Portal

Once login credentials arrive from SIH, the team leader must log in and verify/complete each item below:

1
Verify Pre-Entered Team Name
Ensure the team name matches your internal registration accurately.
2
College Authorization Letter (PDF)
Verify the pre-entered authorization letter in official SIH format.
3
Names of Leader & 5 Members
Verify correct spellings of all 6 team members as per ID proof.
4
Gender of Leader & 5 Members
Confirm mandatory criteria of at least one female member.
5
Email IDs of All 6 Members
Verify active, accessible email addresses for official alerts.
6
Mobile Numbers of All 6 Members
Verify active WhatsApp and calling phone numbers.
7
Chosen PS / Student Innovation
Select up to 2 Problem Statements from the live SIH portal.
8
Idea Title
Enter a clear, professional, and impactful project title.
9
Idea Description
Describe proposed solution, tech stack, novelty & feasibility.
10
Idea Presentation (PDF)
Upload solution PPT converted strictly to PDF format.
⚠️

Waitlisted Teams: Please keep your idea description, presentation, and team details ready. If any shortlisted team withdraws or fails to submit, waitlisted teams will be promoted in order of merit.

Official updates & announcements will continue to be shared through official UIT WhatsApp groups.
