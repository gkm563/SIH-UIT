Instructions for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Internal Registration

Welcome to the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Internal Registration Portal of United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj.

Please read the following guidelines regarding the internal selection process.

Important Instructions

  1. This registration was for the college-level internal evaluation process for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
  2. Submission of internal registration does not guarantee selection for SIH 2026. Teams will be shortlisted through the college's internal hackathon evaluation process.
  3. Official SIH 2026 Problem Statements will be released soon. Registered teams will select problem statements and prepare for internal evaluation.
  4. SIH 2026 mandates a team size of exactly 6 members (1 Team Leader + 5 Members).
  5. Each team must include at least one female member as per mandatory SIH 2026 guidelines.
  6. The Team Leader is responsible for all team communications and problem statement submission.
  7. Ensure that the details of all team members are verified. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in rejection during screening.
  8. Each student should be a member of only one team across the college.
  9. Use a valid and active Personal Email ID and WhatsApp Number, as all official updates will be shared through these channels.
  10. Verify all information carefully. Once submitted, modifications are not allowed.
  11. The college internal registration deadline (05 August 2026, 11:59 PM) has passed. Registrations are currently closed.
  12. The information collected through this portal is used for:
    • Internal registration and participant verification.
    • Team validation and mentor allocation.
    • College-level internal hackathon evaluation.
    • Nomination of Top 50 Shortlisted + 45 Waiting List Teams to the official SIH Government Portal.
  13. Students are encouraged to regularly visit the official Smart India Hackathon website for latest national updates: https://www.sih.gov.in/

Declaration

By participating in the internal registration, teams confirm that:

  • All information provided is true and accurate.
  • All team members have consented to participate in SIH 2026.
  • The team agrees to follow the rules and guidelines issued by the college and Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
  • The team understands that final nomination is subject to the college's internal evaluation process and official SIH guidelines.

We wish all participants the very best for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
