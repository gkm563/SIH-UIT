Official Top 50 Selection List Announcement — Coming Soon

Notice

The finalized list of 45 Shortlisted Teams + 5 Waitlisted Teams is under final verification and will be published shortly across official UIT WhatsApp groups & Email.

👉 Only the Top 50 nominated teams will be uploaded to the central SIH portal and receive official Team Leader login credentials.

1 SPOC Upload

SPOC Data Upload

College SPOC uploads nominated team data & authorization letters to the central SIH portal.

Institutional nomination only
2 Credentials

Login Credentials

Team Leader receives portal Login ID & Password directly from SIH on registered Email / Mobile.

Check Email & SMS inbox
3 Select PS

Select Up to 2 PS

Team Leader logs in on SIH portal and selects up to 2 Problem Statements from sih.gov.in.

Software or Hardware track
4 Submit Idea

Verify & Submit Idea

Verify 10 pre-entered details and submit idea presentation (PDF) before 500-quota freeze!

Early submission recommended
⏳ 500 Ideas Limit / PS Freeze

Only 500 ideas will be accepted nationwide per Problem Statement. Once 500 submissions are reached, that PS gets immediately frozen.

🎯 Max 2 Problem Statements

One nominated team can submit ideas against a maximum of 2 Problem Statements (or Student Innovation Category).

🔑 SPOC Nomination Only

Students cannot register directly. College SPOC handles nominations. The Nominated Team Leader receives portal credentials via Email / SMS.
